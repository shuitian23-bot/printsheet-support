Common questions

How do I print photos in different sizes?

Choose mixed size and set a width and height for each photo, or follow the global default. Each dimension must be 5–350 mm. Set 1–12 copies per photo. Frames keep their orientation; they are not rotated or shrunk to fit. If a frame cannot fit inside the margins, change its size or paper settings. The layout is not guaranteed to use the fewest pages.

These dimensions describe photo frames. Fit can leave white space. Read the mixed-size printing guide.

What is the cut worksheet?

Export the cut worksheet as a separate PDF for page and item numbers, names, dimensions and positions. Its diagram is for reference, not calibration. Re-export both PDFs after a layout or photo change. Print at 100% and measure a test print; the worksheet does not compensate for a printer.

Do saved projects and templates keep individual sizes?

Existing projects can still open with their original sizing mode. New project saves include per-photo sizes. Individual sizes apply in mixed mode; full-page templates ignore these overrides.

How do I start?

Tap Try demo to use the sample, or add up to 12 photos with the system photo picker or Files. Choose A4 or US Letter and an orientation. Arrange your photos, create the PDF, then use Share / Save PDF to save it to Files or send it with another app.

Which sizing mode should I use?

Exact size keeps the width and height you enter for every frame. Automatic prioritizes fewer pages, then compares the photo group's proportions to the available paper area. Horizontal, vertical and fixed-column layouts offer other arrangements; compact starts at the top left. Frames that cannot fit produce an error.

Full-page template adjusts frames to use the area inside the paper margins. Choose automatic or 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9 or 12 photos per page. Automatic uses up to six per page. Rows share their available width and the last page rearranges its remaining photos, so frame dimensions can differ. Three photos in two columns produce two frames above one wider frame below. Automatic compares proportions; it is not an AI guarantee of the best crop.

Why is there white space, or why are edges missing?

Fit preserves the entire image and may leave white space. Fill crops to cover the frame. Zooming out or moving an individually adjusted photo can create white space too. The frame dimensions describe the frame, not necessarily the visible photo content in Fit mode.

How do I change one photo?

Tap it on the paper, or select Adjust below its thumbnail. Pinch to zoom from 0.25× to 5× and drag to move. A slider and direction buttons are also available. Show whole photo sets Fit at 1× and centers the image. Restore global default removes the individual adjustment. Cancel discards your draft; Done applies it. Copies of one source share the adjustment, while different photos remain independent.

Changing the global Fit / Fill default affects photos without individual adjustments. Changing layouts reuses saved adjustments in new frame shapes; compare the preview again. An adjustment that changes the result invalidates the previous PDF, so generate a new one.

How do complete print projects differ from settings templates?

Use Print projects to name and save the current job. A project keeps up to 12 JPEG working copies with crops, per-photo copy overrides, paper and layout settings, and an optional measurement record. Up to 20 projects can be saved, each limited to 80 MiB. Open a saved project to continue or reprint after restarting. Opening or starting a new project warns about unsaved changes. Deleting a project removes its saved copy; a currently open working session remains independent.

Can each photo have a different quantity?

Yes. Choose Follow default or 1–12 copies below each photo. The global copies setting is the default for photos without an override. Print check lists each photo’s actual quantity, lowest effective PPI across its frames, and whether content is cropped or there is white space. These are review prompts; they do not automatically change your crop or certify print quality.

What do saved templates contain?

Up to 20 named templates store global settings, including sizing mode, layout, paper, copies and photos per page. They do not store your photos or their individual zoom and position adjustments. These named settings templates are different from the built-in layout choices.

Why does a photo look soft?

PrintSheet warns when an image's effective resolution falls below 200 PPI. That estimate uses its actual drawing size, including zoom. A warning is not a substitute for a test print. Each input is limited to 40 MB and 50 megapixels. Working JPEG copies have a maximum 4,096-pixel edge; originals are unchanged.

Can I use it offline? Where are my photos?

Photo layout and PDF generation run locally. PrintSheet does not upload your photos or generated PDFs to its servers. Files stored only in iCloud or another provider may first need a system download. Choosing a sharing destination may send your PDF to that service under its own terms.

Unsaved working photos and generated PDFs are temporary. Save a complete print project to keep its JPEG working copies, individual crops, quantities, settings and measurement record on this device. Saved projects survive app restarts; save exported PDFs separately to Files. Settings templates still contain settings only.

My printed dimensions are wrong. What should I check?

Create and save a test-ruler PDF with the same paper and orientation. Print at 100% / Actual Size, disable Fit to Page and other automatic scaling, then measure with a physical ruler. Check the paper setting in both your PDF software and printer driver. Increase margins if your printer clips edges. You may enter your physical measurement of the 100 mm line, with a note, paper, orientation and date. The record does not change PDF dimensions or automatically compensate for a printer. Recheck after changing printer, software, paper or scaling settings. PDF dimensions do not guarantee paper output.

Does this certify passport or ID photos?

No. PrintSheet is a photo-layout tool for printing and crafts. It does not verify government photo requirements or certify identity-photo compliance.

Are usage statistics required?

Statistics are off by default. Choose Allow or Not now at the first prompt, or change the switch in Help later; your choice persists after restarting. Declining does not affect layout or PDF export. If allowed, fixed feature events, quantity/time ranges, app metadata, random event IDs and a process-only session UUID go to app-metrics.shuitian13.chatgpt.site. There is no persistent installation ID or advertising ID and no cross-app identity. Photos, PDFs, filenames, entered text, template names and raw exception messages are excluded. Turning the switch off stops future optional uploads; already received raw events follow a 90-day periodic cleanup rule. Read the privacy policy for infrastructure processing and Apple reports.

Need a hand?

Email shuitian13@gmail.com with your device, iOS and app versions, paper size, and what happened. Remove personal information from screenshots. You do not need to send original photos.

Usage statistics are optional and off by default. Learn about the fields, your switch and retention in the privacy policy.

常见问题

如何混排不同尺寸的照片？

选择混合尺寸，为每张照片设置宽高或跟随全局默认；每个尺寸为 5–350 毫米，每张可设 1–12 份。照片框保持指定方向，不自动旋转或缩小；放不进边距内时，请调整尺寸或纸张设置。布局不保证页数全局最少。

尺寸指照片框；完整模式可能留白。查看混排实操指南。

裁切作业单有什么用？

裁切作业单可另存为 PDF，列出页号、序号、名称、尺寸与位置。示意图仅供参考，不用于校准。更改布局或照片后请重新导出两份 PDF。打印时选 100% 并实测；作业单不会自动补偿打印机误差。

项目和模板会保留逐图尺寸吗？

旧项目仍可按原尺寸模式打开；新保存的项目包含逐图尺寸。逐图尺寸仅在混合模式生效，整页模板会忽略这些覆盖值。

如何开始？

点击“试用示例”，或从系统照片选择器、“文件”添加最多 12 张照片。选择 A4 或 US Letter 和纸张方向，排版后生成 PDF，再通过“分享 / 保存 PDF”保存至“文件”或发送到其他 App。

精确尺寸和整页模板有什么区别？

精确尺寸让每个照片框保持输入宽高。自动布局先尽量省页，再比较照片组与纸张区域比例；还可选择横排、竖排、两列、三列，或从左上排列的紧凑布局。框放不进纸张或指定布局时会提示。

整页模板会调整照片框以利用边距内区域。每页数量可选自动或 1、2、3、4、6、9、12 张，自动时每页最多 6 张。每行均分全宽，最后一页按剩余张数重排，框尺寸因此可能不同。三张照片选两列时，上面两张、下面一张更宽。自动推荐按比例比较，不是 AI 最优裁切保证。

为什么有白边，或照片边缘不见了？

完整模式保留全图，比例不同时可能留白；铺满模式裁切以覆盖尺寸框。单图缩小或移动也可能产生白边。输入的精确尺寸描述照片框，不一定等于完整模式中照片内容实际占用的尺寸。

如何调整一张照片？

点击主纸张中的照片或缩略图下的“调整”。双指可在 0.25–5 倍之间缩放，拖动可移动，也可使用滑块和方向按钮。“完整显示”设为完整模式、1 倍、居中；“恢复全局默认”移除该图独立调整。“取消”不写回，“完成”才应用。同一来源的所有打印份数共用调整，不同照片互不影响。

全局完整 / 铺满只影响未独立调整的图。换布局后调整会用于新框比例，请重新检查预览。保存的改动若改变结果，旧 PDF 会失效，需要重新生成。

完整打印项目与设置模板有何不同？

在“打印项目”中命名并保存当前作品。项目保存最多 12 张 JPEG 工作副本、裁切、逐图份数、纸张和布局设置，以及可选测量记录；最多保存 20 个项目，每项上限 80 MiB。重启后打开项目可继续调整或重印。打开其他项目、新建项目前会提示未保存修改；删除项目会移除其保存副本，当前打开的工作会话独立保留。

每张照片可以设置不同份数吗？

可以。在缩略图下选择“跟随默认”或 1–12 份；全局份数作为未单独设置照片的默认值。“打印检查”逐图显示实际份数、所有照片框中的最低有效 PPI，以及裁边、留白提示。这些提示不会自动改变裁切，也不代表已验证纸面质量。

保存的模板包含什么？

最多 20 个命名设置模板，保存模式、布局、纸张、份数、每页数量等全局设置，不保存照片及其缩放、位置。命名的设置模板与内置布局选项不同，不会恢复之前会话的照片。

为什么照片可能不清晰？

App 会对低于 200 PPI 的情况给出提示，按图片实际绘制尺寸及缩放计算。提示不能替代试印。单图上限为 40 MB、5,000 万像素；导入后转为最长边不超过 4,096 像素的 JPEG 工作副本，原文件不变。

能离线使用吗？照片存在哪里？

排版和 PDF 生成在本机完成，PrintSheet 不会把照片或生成的 PDF 上传到其服务器。仅存于 iCloud 或第三方网盘的原件可能需要系统先下载；选择分享目的地后，该服务按自身规则处理 PDF。

未保存的工作照片和导出 PDF 是临时文件。保存完整打印项目后，JPEG 工作副本、单图裁切、份数、设置及测量记录会保留在本机，重启 App 后可以打开恢复。导出的 PDF 仍需另存到“文件”；设置模板依然只包含参数。

打印出来尺寸不对怎么办？

先按相同纸型和方向生成测试尺 PDF，打印时选择 100% / 实际大小，关闭“适合页面”及其他自动缩放，用实体尺测量。同时核对 PDF 软件及打印驱动的纸张设置；边缘被裁时可增加边距。可记录 100 mm 测试线的纸面实测长度、备注、纸型、方向和日期。记录不会改变 PDF 尺寸，也不自动补偿打印机误差；更换打印机、软件、纸张或缩放设置后应重新测量。PDF 文档尺寸不保证纸面结果。

可以认证护照或证件照吗？

本工具用于照片打印和手工排版，不核验政府证件照片要求，不提供证件照合规认证。
