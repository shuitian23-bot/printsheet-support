PrintSheet

Print photos in different sizes

Make a mixed-size photo PDF and a separate cut worksheet.

Steps

Choose paper and photos

Add up to 12 photos, then choose paper, orientation and margins.

Set sizes and copies

Choose mixed size. Set each photo frame to 5–350 mm in width and height, or follow the global default. Choose 1–12 copies per photo.

Review and export

Check the preview, export the photo PDF, then export the separate cut worksheet PDF. The worksheet lists page and item numbers, names, dimensions and positions.

Print and measure

Print at 100% / Actual Size with automatic page scaling off. Measure a test print with a physical ruler and adjust your printer settings as needed.

Changing photos or layout invalidates earlier exports; generate both PDFs again. Up to 20 projects can be saved, each up to 80 MiB. Optional statistics are off by default.

Questions

Will the layout rotate or shrink a frame to fit?

No. Mixed-size frames keep their specified orientation and are not rotated or shrunk. If a frame cannot fit inside the margins, adjust its size or paper settings. The layout does not guarantee the fewest pages.

Why is there white space?

Dimensions describe the photo frame. Fit preserves the whole image and may leave white space; Fill crops to cover the frame.

Can the worksheet calibrate my printer?

No. Its diagram is for reference, not calibration. Printer and paper output must be checked with a physical test print; the app does not automatically compensate for printer error.

What happens to older projects and templates?

Older projects open with their original sizing mode. New project saves include individual sizes. Individual size overrides apply in mixed mode; full-page templates ignore them.
